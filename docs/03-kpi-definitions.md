## How costs are assigned to trips

- **Fuel:** the month's fuel spend, split across that month's trips by gallons burned. Fuel purchases aren't reliably linked to trips (29% more gallons bought than burned).
- **Maintenance:** a truck's cost in a month, split across its trips that month by miles. Maintenance of trucks that ran no trip that month is *Unattributed*.
- **Incidents:** directly by trip.
- **Driver pay:** not in the data, so not included. Contribution is before driver pay.

## Not measured, and why

- **Idle time:** `idle_time_hours` is random noise (correlation ≈ 0 with trip duration, distance and fuel).
- **Fuel price by location:** prices differ by only $0.02/gallon across cities.
- **By facility:** `facility_id` on delivery events is essentially random; `location_city` is used instead.
